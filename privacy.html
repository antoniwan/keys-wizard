The app About Privacy

Privacy

Keys Wizard counts visits, but it does not collect data that identifies you. Your songs, your notes, and your microphone sound stay in your browser.

Last change: November 15, 2026.

The short version

  • There are no accounts.
  • There are no ads.
  • There are no cookies.
  • The site counts visits and button clicks. It does not use cookies for this and does not follow you to other sites.
  • The app does not upload your song files.
  • The app does not record or send sound from your microphone.

What stays on your computer

The app keeps these things in your browser. They do not go to a server.

What Where Why
The song files that you open Browser storage (IndexedDB) To show them in “Your songs” the next time
Your settings Browser storage (localStorage) To remember the keyboard size, the volume, and the mode
The notes from your MIDI keyboard Memory, while the page is open To know which key you played
The sound from your microphone Memory, while the microphone is on To find the pitch of the note. The sound is not saved.

Permissions

The browser asks for your permission before the app can use a MIDI keyboard or the microphone. The microphone is off until you select the microphone button in the top bar. You can remove a permission in the settings of your browser at any time.

Visit counts

I want to know if people use the app, and which parts they use. For this, the live site uses two counters. Neither one sets a cookie, and neither one sees your songs, your notes, or your microphone.

  • Stats is my own copy of Umami, an open-source counter. It runs on Vercel. It receives the page address, the site that sent you, your browser, your operating system, your screen size, your language, and your country. It does not keep your IP address. It also counts clicks on some buttons, for example "Open song" or a free song. For a song in "Your songs", it counts only the click, not the name of the song. It counts links to other sites by their address. If your browser sends "Do Not Track", Stats does not count you.
  • Vercel Web Analytics receives the page address, the site that sent you, your browser, your operating system, your device type, and your country. To count visitors, it uses a code made from your request that changes every day. Read how Vercel Web Analytics uses data.

I see only totals. I do not sell or share this data.

What the host can see

Vercel hosts this site. When your browser gets a page, the Vercel servers receive the usual technical data of a web request, such as your IP address, the page address, and the name of your browser. Vercel can keep this data in its logs. I do not use it to identify you. Read the Vercel privacy policy.

The page does not load fonts or pictures from other companies. The only script from a different address is the Stats counter. Links to other sites, such as GitHub, open only when you select them.

How to remove your data

  • To remove one song: on the start page, select ✕ next to the song.
  • To remove all data: in your browser, clear the site data for this site.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. It does not ask for a name, an email address, or other personal data.

Changes

If the app starts to use data in a different way, I will change this page first and put the new date at the top. The history of this page is public on GitHub.
